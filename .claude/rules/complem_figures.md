# Complementary Analysis (`complem_figures/`)

Scripts in `complem_figures/` for visualization and statistics. Most figures are drawn at their printed size (`--width-cm`, default 16 cm = `\linewidth`) and written as both a 300 dpi PNG and a vector PDF, so the report includes them at scale 1. **Every reader of a `RESULT/*.csv` defaults to the PDF expectation** (`expect_lat`/`expect_lon`/`expect_z`); `--solution maxlike` switches to the maximum-likelihood point through `event_maps.hypocentre_columns`, which reads `maxlike_*` and falls back to `latitude`/`longitude`/`depth` on a CSV written before `SAVE_NLLOC_EXPECTATION` (where those *are* the maximum-likelihood point). `cross_section.py` is the exception and still reads `latitude`/`longitude`/`depth`.
- `event_maps.py` — PyGMT map of seismicity coloured by depth; optional zone boxes (`--region-in/--region-out`, used by `generate_complem_maps.py`), stations, and cross-section overlays (`--profile`, `--profiles-default` for Aneto/Arette/Andorra) whose swath is the exact strip `cross_section.py` projects
- `profile_location_map.py` — location map of cross-section profiles (labelled X–X′, towns, whole-range inset), reusing `event_maps`' reader and profile geometry
- `aoi_map.py` — the AOI half-planes of `global_obs/filter_events_by_aoi.py` over the already-filtered RESIF and IGN+ICGC epicentres of `obs/`; read-only, never re-filters a bulletin
- `source_depth_maps.py` — two source bulletins side by side (IGN / LDG by default), coloured by depth and sized by magnitude
- `network_history.py` — seismicity per year, stations seen per year, and the temporary deployments on one time axis
- `magnitude_regression.py` — the three magnitude-conversion regressions of `global_obs/generate_magnitude_models.py` as one three-panel figure; needs the pre-conversion header tables (`--extract-from` once, from the archived bulletins)
- `gutenberg_richter.py` — magnitude-frequency distribution
- `depth_maps.py` — depth distribution
- `depth_histogram.py` — histogram of event depths (.obs, or a result CSV); `depth_histogram_compare.py` — the same events before relocation / expectation / maximum likelihood, three stacked panels matched on `publicId`
- `error_maps.py` — windowed-median ERH/ERV maps, batch per `--time-range`-year period or one `--start`/`--end` page; events at the expectation; the median is computed from events binned once (identical to the former per-cell loop, checked on ~3 000 cells), so a page takes seconds. A light grey hillshade (sea very pale blue) sits under the grid by default, `--no-dem` removes it; with it the cells go opaque (0.9 alpha without), since `rocket_r` and the hillshade both encode lightness. The relief is `basemap_lines.draw_relief`, reading `complem_figures/basemap/relief_15s.npz` (GMT `earth_relief_15s`, gitignored), written by the same `basemap_lines.py --dump` as the borders. Sea = DEM ≤ 0 m, not the shoreline
- `error_vs_quality.py` — published ERH/ERZ against RMS, gap, Nphs and nearest-station distance, the four-column version of `pdf_metrics.py`'s error figure (its statistics are imported, not copied)
- `cross_section.py` — vertical cross-sections; `--format 6` reads `RESULT/SSST_result.csv` directly (`true_erh`/`true_erz` as the error filter), colours the section by `--use-err erh|erv|psi|c68z`, and `--usable` applies the same acceptance test as `pyr:usable` in the QuakeML export
- `cross_section_panels.py` — several depth sections of one profile side by side (default Aneto: before relocation / NLL / NLL-SSST), 1:1 scale, one shared colour bar
- `station_map.py` — map of seismic stations
- `zone_map.py` — overview map of the 6 NLL zones, rebuilt from `run/nll_loc/GLOBAL_<N>/last.in` (needs stage 4; raises when none is found)
- `event_ranking.py` — ranks events by pdfVolume/ellipsoidVolume change (NLL → SSST); flags multi-zone/zone-changed events; carries the PDF-quality columns (post-SSST only) when `pdf_metrics.py` has annotated the CSV, and runs without them otherwise; optional gridmap PDF
- `plot_pdf_cloud.py` — interactive 3D PDF scatter-cloud of one event across SSST iterations (Plotly). Each ellipsoid is centred on the PDF expectation, which is also the published hypocentre; the diamond markers and the path joining them are the **maximum-likelihood** point, read from the `.hyp` `MAXIMUM_LIKELIHOOD` line. The gap between a diamond and its ellipsoid centre is that iteration's mode-vs-mean distance. Its NLL reference panel needs the `maxlike_*` columns in `RESULT/NLL_result.csv`
- `plot_pdf_cloud_2d.py` — static 2-D counterpart for one iteration: map view + vertical section along the ellipsoid's widest horizontal direction, with the *projected* (not sliced) ellipsoid; imports its `.hyp` helpers from `plot_pdf_cloud.py`
- `ssst_evolution.py` — per-zone pdfVolume/EllipsoidLen3/RMS evolution across SSST iterations (convergence QC)
- `seismicity_3d.py` — interactive 3-D map (self-contained HTML, Plotly.js inlined, ~16 MB) of all three location stages: pre-reloc (`obs/GLOBAL.obs`), NLL and SSST, one at a time with the camera kept. **Each stage shows all its own events** (57 856 / 50 918 / 46 224), so events lost between stages are part of what changes. Colour = depth, every dot 1.5 px; magnitude (from `GLOBAL.obs`, joined on `publicId`) feeds the hover, panel and filter only; borders/shorelines from the `basemap_lines` cache and GTSRCE stations at z = 0. Clicking an event dims the rest (scatter3d has no per-point alpha, so the whole trace goes to a slider-set opacity, default 0.08, and the event is redrawn on its own), shows its metadata at all three stages with the displacements, and draws its pre→NLL→SSST trajectory; "Auto-zoom" aims the camera at the solutions' centroid, keeps the viewing direction, and backs off until their projected spread covers half the panel (from Plotly's own data→scene mapping and 45° perspective; floor of a 1 km sphere). Filters: years, minimum magnitude, usable only — `export_quakeml.classify` itself, so 45 356 on today's CSV, not `FINAL.xml`'s 45 415, which predates the 0.68 → 0.6827 change. Vertical-exaggeration slider. `seisbench_env`, ~5 s
- `station_colocation.py` — the one diagnostic aimed at **stage 1**: is 20 m the right co-location radius? Reads the 2 414 pre-merge stations (`stations/*/*.xml`) against the groups in `GLOBAL_inventory.xml`. Separations are bimodal with a real trough between 20 m and 200 m, so the threshold does sit in a gap, but it is not free: 651 pairs within 20 m, 811 within 30 m, the extras inside the dense `AM`/`8M`/`XI` nodal arrays. "Merged" is read from the **outcome**, not from the distance — which is what exposes the **423 pairs that merged from beyond the radius** through the single-linkage chaining of `_combine_close_stations`, and the 38 inside it that did not because `check_inventory` removed one by hand. Three panels (nearest-neighbour split merged/not, threshold sensitivity, per-network), `seisbench_env`, ~20 s, → `complem_figures/station_colocation/`
- `temporary_network_impact.py` — what the external picks of §5's `add_temp_picks.py` bought, from the one place the question is cleanly answerable: `obs/NLL_result.obs` and `obs/NLL_result_augmented.obs` hold the **same 50 918 events with byte-identical hypocentres** — their event lines differ in `PhaseCount` alone — so anything that moves between them is the added picks, with no relocation mixed in. That is why it does **not** compare `NLL_result.csv` against `SSST_result.csv`, which would confound the two (different event sets, and hypocentres moved ~1.33 km median). Two figures in `complem_figures/temporary_network_impact/`, `seisbench_env`, ~5 s:
  - `phase_count_timeline.png/.pdf` — two chronological strips (a, b) 1978→2025 coloured by the median phases per event per month, before above and after below on a **shared** colour scale (that sharing is the figure). Empty months (8 of 565) stay grey rather than closing the gap. The default `vmax` is the p99 of the pooled monthly medians because exactly one bin in 1 114 reaches 52 and would otherwise eat half the ramp; the colorbar declares the clip with `extend='max'`. Median 12 → 15 phases overall, but the gain is **entirely post-2010** — only 585 of the 17 077 pre-2010 events gain anything
  - `nearest_station_distance.pdf` — overlapping before/after histograms of the epicentral distance to the closest recording station, all events beside the 26 150 that gained picks. Median 10.80 → 9.89 km, and events with a station within 5 km go 14.7 % → 19.3 % (14.1 % → 23.0 % on the gained-picks panel). Distances beyond the axis limit fold into the last bin, but every printed statistic is computed on the **unclipped** values

  Station positions come from `stations/GTSRCE_*.txt`, i.e. the coordinates NLLoc itself located with, so the result is checkable against the binary: the computed median reproduces NLLoc's own `Dist` column to **0.02 km**. One pick in 1 020 452 sits on a code absent from those files (`FR.0002`) and is reported, never silently dropped.
- `ssst_corrections.py` — see `.claude/rules/ssst_corrections.md`

`complem_figures/pdf_metrics/` holds figures too, but they are written by `NLL_run/pdf_metrics.py` (see above), not by a module living here.

`event_ranking.py`, `plot_pdf_cloud.py`, `ssst_evolution.py` and `seismicity_3d.py` are standalone diagnostics, not wired into the two driver scripts above; they read `RESULT/*.csv` and `run/nll_loc/` / `run/ssst_loc/<run-name>/` directly. `station_colocation.py` is standalone too, but reads `stations/` rather than any relocation output, and `temporary_network_impact.py` likewise, reading only `obs/NLL_result*.obs` and `stations/GTSRCE_*.txt`.

`zone_Arette/` — focused analysis of the Arette seismic zone.
